CAREER GUIDANCE

Explore Career Paths

These summaries are starting points. Always check official recruitment and college information before making decisions.

🎖️ Army Careers

Serve the nation through different officer and other-rank entry routes.

  • Eligibility depends on the entry route, age, education and medical fitness.
  • After Class 10, some entries may be available; officer routes usually require further study or specific qualifications.
  • Training, selection tests and medical standards apply.
  • Uniform and responsibilities vary by rank and role.

Pay: Salary depends on rank, entry type, allowances and service conditions.

Pay figures change. Check the official Indian Army recruitment notice for the exact entry and current pay.
Official Army Recruitment →

🩺 Doctor Careers

Help people maintain health and treat illness through medical science.

  • School science subjects are important for the usual MBBS route.
  • MBBS admission in India generally requires NEET-UG and meeting current eligibility rules.
  • MBBS study and internship take several years; specialisation requires more study.
  • Other healthcare careers include nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy and lab technology.

Pay: Depends on qualification, employer, location, experience and specialisation.

There is no single doctor salary. Government, private, early-career and specialist salaries can differ substantially.
NEET Official Website →

💼 Other Job Options

There are many routes to a career. Compare education, interests, costs and daily responsibilities.

  • Government services and public-sector roles.
  • Engineering, IT and cybersecurity.
  • Teaching, banking, business and skilled trades.
  • Healthcare, design, media and creative work.

Pay: Varies widely by role, qualification, recruitment level and experience.

Avoid choosing a career based only on salary. Check the course cost, eligibility, job stability and your interests.
